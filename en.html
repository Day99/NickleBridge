How it works

From requirement to delivery coordination

Each step is reviewed by our commercial team. A potential match is never presented as a confirmed deal.

  1. 1

    Requirement

    Buyer submits an RFQ: ore type, grade, chemistry, volume, delivery terms and timing.

  2. 2

    Sourcing

    We identify registered and prospective suppliers with ore that can meet the specification.

  3. 3

    Verification

    Supplier IUP, RKAB status and COA are collected and reviewed for consistency.

  4. 4

    Matching

    Suitable suppliers are shortlisted against chemistry, volume, location and logistics.

  5. 5

    Introduction

    Both parties are introduced under an agreed brokerage arrangement and negotiate directly.

  6. 6

    Coordination

    We support communication through sampling, contracting and delivery scheduling.

Sourcing regions

Focused on Indonesia’s nickel belt

Our sourcing effort concentrates on the provinces where laterite mining and smelting are established.

Focus regions

  • Central SulawesiHome to large integrated industrial parks around Morowali and a major concentration of RKEF and HPAL capacity.
  • Southeast SulawesiLong-established laterite mining around Kolaka, Pomalaa and Konawe, with ore and smelting activity along the coast.
  • South SulawesiNickel mining and processing in East Luwu, including the Sorowako area.
  • North MalukuGrowing nickel processing on Halmahera and Obi Island, served by sea logistics.
Explore the network

  • Mine

    Laterite ore is mined in benches and graded at the stockpile.

  • Jetty loading

    Ore is hauled to the jetty and loaded onto barges by conveyor or excavator.

  • Barging

    Loaded barges carry ore by sea to smelter ports.

About NickelBridge

A sourcing and brokerage network for Indonesian nickel ore

NickelBridge is a B2B nickel ore sourcing and brokerage network operated by PT Maleo Indonesia Bulava. We connect smelters and industrial buyers with legally authorised nickel ore producers, and coordinate the commercial process between them.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is NickelBridge?

+

NickelBridge is a B2B nickel ore sourcing and brokerage network operated by PT Maleo Indonesia Bulava. It connects legally authorised nickel ore producers in Indonesia with smelters and industrial buyers.

Does NickelBridge sell nickel ore directly?

+

No. NickelBridge is a commercial intermediary. It does not own mines or sell ore on its own account. Buyers and licensed suppliers negotiate, contract and transact directly with each other.

Which nickel ore types does NickelBridge source?

+

Saprolite nickel ore (typical indicative Ni 1.5%–2.0%, mainly for RKEF smelters) and limonite nickel ore (typical indicative Ni 0.8%–1.5%, mainly for HPAL plants). Actual specifications are confirmed per cargo through supplier documents and independent testing.

Which regions does NickelBridge cover?

+

The sourcing focus is Indonesia’s nickel belt: Central Sulawesi (including Morowali), Southeast Sulawesi, South Sulawesi and North Maluku.

How can a smelter or buyer request nickel ore?

+

Submit an RFQ on the For Buyers page with ore type, required Ni grade, Fe, MgO and SiO₂ specifications, maximum moisture, volume in WMT per month, delivery location and delivery terms.

What documents do nickel ore suppliers need?

+

Suppliers register with their company profile, IUP documentation, current RKAB evidence and a certificate of analysis (COA). Documents are reviewed before any introduction and are never published.

How does NickelBridge earn revenue?

+

NickelBridge earns a brokerage or introduction commission that is agreed in writing with the relevant party before an introduction is made.

How do I contact NickelBridge?
