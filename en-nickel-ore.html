Nickel ore

Saprolite and limonite nickel ore

Indonesian laterite ore falls into two main types with different chemistry and processing routes. The parameters below define what we match on; actual values are confirmed per cargo.

Saprolite Nickel Ore

Typical indicative Ni range
1.5% – 2.0% Ni
Typical processing route
RKEF → nickel pig iron (NPI) / ferronickel

Lower-profile laterite with higher nickel and magnesium content and lower iron. The primary feed for rotary kiln–electric furnace (RKEF) smelters.

Limonite Nickel Ore

Typical indicative Ni range
0.8% – 1.5% Ni
Typical processing route
HPAL → mixed hydroxide precipitate (MHP)

Upper-profile laterite with high iron and low magnesium content. Commonly processed by high-pressure acid leaching (HPAL).

Nickel ore

The laterite profile

Laterite nickel forms as ultramafic rock weathers in tropical conditions. Iron-rich limonite accumulates near the surface; magnesium-rich saprolite lies beneath it, above the parent bedrock.
